About

Three disciplines, one direction.

Business, code, and creativity aren't separate lanes for me — they're one practice, applied from different angles.

I'm Somnath Roy — I work at the intersection of technology and business. By day, I operate as a Business Development Executive in US IT Staffing, where I build client relationships, run lead generation, and manage the full sales cycle from first outreach to closed placement. Alongside that, I design and ship full stack web products, and I founded Dream Wish Studio, a studio building premium, personal digital experiences.

My journey started with curiosity about how things are built — first the web, then the businesses that run on it. That curiosity turned into a habit of learning in public: shipping real projects, picking up frameworks as I need them, and treating every client conversation as a chance to understand a problem more deeply before I try to solve it.

My philosophy is simple: build things that feel considered. Whether that's a staffing pipeline, a line of code, or a website someone will open on their wedding day, the standard doesn't change.
